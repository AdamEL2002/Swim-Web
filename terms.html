Bookings & Payment

Please only make payment once your lesson time and start date have been confirmed by Barnet Premier Swim. A booking is only confirmed once we have agreed the lesson details with you and payment has been received.

Our Responsibilities

We will provide swimming lessons with instructors who are qualified through STA, Swim England, or an equivalent recognised swimming teaching body. Instructors may teach from in or out of the water and may support swimmers physically where appropriate to help with body position, confidence, safety, and correct stroke technique.

If one of our regular instructors is unavailable, we may provide a suitably qualified cover instructor. We will keep to the agreed lesson format and swimmer numbers wherever possible. Swimming days and times are subject to availability and cannot be guaranteed, as sessions may become fully booked.

Refunds & Cancellations Policy

If Barnet Premier Swim has to cancel a lesson and we are unable to deliver it, you will receive a credit for that lesson. This may be added to your current booking or arranged as a replacement lesson at a day and time agreed by both parties. Replacement lessons are subject to availability and may not be with your usual instructor or at your usual lesson time.

Once your lesson time and start date have been agreed, that space is reserved for you. If you do not attend a booked lesson, we are unable to offer a refund, credit, or replacement lesson, regardless of the reason for non-attendance.

Late Arrival & Missed Lessons

If a swimmer arrives late, the lesson may still need to finish at the scheduled time. Missed lessons, late arrivals, or non-attendance by the customer are non-refundable and will not be credited.

Safety & Suitability

Please tell us about any medical conditions, additional needs, injuries, or relevant information before lessons begin. Swimmers must follow pool rules and instructor guidance at all times.

Changes To Lessons

We may occasionally need to change lesson times, instructors, or arrangements due to pool availability, safety reasons, or circumstances outside our control. We will give as much notice as reasonably possible.
