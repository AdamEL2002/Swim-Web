Bookings & Payment

Please only make payment once your lesson time and start date have been confirmed by Barnet Premier Swim. A booking is only confirmed once we have agreed the lesson details with you and payment has been received.

Cancellation Policy

Once a lesson time and start date have been confirmed, your booking is reserved for you. Cancellations made with less than 24 hours' notice are non-refundable. Where possible, we will try to rearrange the lesson, but this cannot be guaranteed and is subject to instructor and pool availability.

Late Arrival & Missed Lessons

If a swimmer arrives late, the lesson may still need to finish at the scheduled time. Missed lessons or non-attendance without the required notice are non-refundable.

Safety & Suitability

Please tell us about any medical conditions, additional needs, injuries, or relevant information before lessons begin. Swimmers must follow pool rules and instructor guidance at all times.

Changes To Lessons

We may occasionally need to change lesson times, instructors, or arrangements due to pool availability, safety reasons, or circumstances outside our control. We will give as much notice as reasonably possible.
