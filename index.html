About Us

Meet Our Instructors

Friendly, experienced instructors helping swimmers build confidence, technique, and safety in the water. Both instructors are lifeguard qualified!

Adam

I was a water polo player for Enfield Water Polo Club, and I love teaching swimmers of all ages and levels. My aim is to help each person feel more confident, safe, and relaxed in the water.

Vahid

@swimreels · 15,000+ followers

I am a former competitive swimmer and support swimmers from beginner level through to advanced technique work. I also run swimreels, an Instagram swimming page with over 15,000 followers.

Why Choose Us

Private and Semi-Private Lessons

We offer 1-to-1 and 2-to-1 lessons for swimmers who want focused attention, faster progress, and a calm, tailored approach that works especially well for nervous swimmers.

Quiet Private Venue

Lessons take place in a quieter private school pool, away from the crowds of a busy leisure centre, helping swimmers feel calmer, focused, and more confident.

All Ages and Abilities

Our instructors have experience teaching both children and adults, from first-time swimmers to advanced classes, so every lesson is matched to the swimmer’s level.
